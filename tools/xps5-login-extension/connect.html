Link your X account to XPS5

This browser's x.com session goes to your PS5, so XPS5 opens signed in. No FTP, no files to copy.

  1. On the PS5, open XPS5, then Settings, X account, and press ✕. Or press Link from a PC where XPS5 says the session is missing.
  2. Make sure x.com is open in this browser with the account you want on the PS5.
  3. Scan the PS5's QR and press this extension's button on the page it opens, or type the address and the code the PS5 shows. Then press Link.

As on the PS5's screen, for example 192.168.1.70

Six digits, changed each time the screen opens

Do not sign out of X in this browser afterwards: X then ends the session on the PS5 as well. Closing the tab or the browser is fine.

The extension reads only x.com's auth_token and ct0 cookies, sends them only to the address you type, over your home network, and keeps nothing but that address and the PS5's language.
